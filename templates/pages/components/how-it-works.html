How it works

One link, two outcomes.

Lev Review gives each location a review link that routes customers based on their rating. The public path stays fast, and the recovery path gives your team the details they need.

01

Register each location

Add a Google Place ID once, then keep separate links for each storefront, office, or service area.

02

Share the review link

Use it in receipts, QR codes, email follow-ups, or printed signage. Customers see one simple rating screen.

03

Route the response

High ratings continue to Google. Low ratings open a private form so your team can recover the experience first.
