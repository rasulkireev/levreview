How it works

One link, configurable outcomes.

Start with a simple Google review link for each location. When you need more control, Pro lets you collect private feedback below the rating you choose.

01

Add each business location

Connect the Google listing once, then keep separate links for each storefront, office, or service area.

02

Share one review link

Use it in receipts, QR codes, email follow-ups, or printed signage. Customers see one simple rating screen.

03

Choose how ratings flow

Send customers to Google by default, or use Pro settings to collect private feedback below the rating you choose.
